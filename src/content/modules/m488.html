1. Introduction

In this unit, you will continue to explore the implications of analyticity of a function of a complex variable. In the previous unit, the main implication of analyticity appeared in the form of the Cauchy- Riemann conditions which are partial differential equations connecting the real and imaginary parts of an analytic formation. In this unit, you will see the Cauchy Integral Theorem and the Cauchy integral formulas which are integral relations satisfied by analytic functions. In this unit, you will also study Taylor and Laurent series for analytic functions as well as the concept of residue.
