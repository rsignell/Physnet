\SummaryItem Important Results


Multiplication of a vector by a number: Defn. C1

\(|m\vec{A}| = |m| \cdot |\vec{A}|\)
\(m\vec{A}\) has same direction as \(\vec{A}\) if \(m\) is positive, opposite direction if \(m\) is negative.

Addition and subtraction of vectors: Defn. D2, Defn. E1, \TxtRuleRefE2


Manipulation of vector symbols: \TxtRuleRefE5

Algebraic symbols representing vectors can be manipulated like ordinary numbers in all processes involving the addition or subtraction of such symbols, or their multiplication or division by numbers.
