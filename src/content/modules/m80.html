The Delta Function (Jim O'Connell)

⏱ 1 hour(s)

Learning Objectives

Input Skills

  • 1. Knowledge of Coulomb's Law.
  • 2. Understanding of the Kronecker delta operator.
  • 3. Requires knowledge of Poisson's equations for a point charge, the Hermite polynomials, Parseval's relation, and the quantum mechanical position and momentum operation and their associated wave functions.

Output Skills (Knowledge)

  • K1. List the properties of the delta function as a distribution and in its uses in integral calculus.
  • K2. Show the relationship between the delta function and the Heaviside distribution.
  • K3. Show the relationship between the Kronecker delta and the Dirac delta function.
  • K4. Derive the momentum distribution in quantum mechanics.
