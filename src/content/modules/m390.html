Time-Independent Perturbations (R.Spital)

⏱ 2 hour(s)

Learning Objectives

Input Skills

  • 1. Vocabulary: diagonal matrix elements, off-diagonal matrix elements.
  • 2. Unknown: assume MISN-0-389.

Output Skills (Knowledge)

  • K1. Given any time-independent perturbation \({\cal H}_1\) and any complete set of non-degenerate eigenstates of \({\cal H}_0\), derive: (a) the energy shifts to first and second order in \({\cal H}_1\), (b) the corrections to the wave-functions to first and second order in \({\cal H}_1\); and using the results of (a) and (b) above, write approximate expressions for the eigenvalues and eigenfunctions of \({\cal H}\) to first or second order in \({\cal H}_1\).

Output Skills (Problem Solving)

  • S1. Solve problems such as 9-5, 9-9, 9-10, 9-11 and 9-13.
