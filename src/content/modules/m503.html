1. INTRODUCTION

You have already studied most of the mathematics needed for this course. However, there are probably some of the more formal vector identities (especially those involving the del operator, \(\vec{\nabla}\)) and vector theorems with which you are not too familiar. This unit will review vector analysis and refresh your memory regarding major vector theorems. The major vector theorems are summarized in Table 1-1. You should refer to this table whenever you meet a problem involving the differential operator, \(\vec{\nabla}\).

  1. 1. Read chapter 1 of the text (Reitz and Milford).
  2. 2. Underline in the text or write out the definitions and explanations of the terms in Output Skill K1.
  3. 3. Write down the various gradients, divergence, and curl called for in Output Skills R1 and R2. In particular,
  4. gradient in rectangular coordinates (eq. 1-20)

  8. 4. Write down and memorize these theorems:
  9. Divergence Theorem (p. 13 and eq. 1-37)
  10. Stoke's Theorem (p. 16 and eq. 1-45)
  11. Green's Theorem (eq. 1-57)
  12. 5. Solve the following problems in Reitz and Milford using Table 1-1 and 1-2 where appropriate:
  13. Problem 1-2, 1-16, 1-19, 1-20, 1-22, and 1-26.
